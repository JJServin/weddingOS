# Product principles

WeddingOS helps people hear themselves, understand their partner, converse well, identify ambiguity and make their own grounded decisions. It never decides whether a couple should marry or wait.

The questionnaire may exist backstage, but the couple should experience a guided conversation. Private preparation precedes shared conversation; sharing is explicit; silence, prayer and pauses are valid; understanding—not agreement or completion—is success. No choice is scored, ranked or treated as more faithful.
