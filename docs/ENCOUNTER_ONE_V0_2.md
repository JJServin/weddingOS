# Encounter One v0.2 — What Are We Saying Yes To?

## Implementation plan
The revision versions storage, expands structured private responses, replaces private activities, updates permission-aware sharing and bridge rules, rebuilds shared conversation steps, adds simultaneous reveal, revises capture/integration/record/feedback, removes former timeline framing, and validates privacy, accessibility and mobile behavior.

## Purpose and research principles
Encounter One helps partners name what marriage means, imagine ordinary shared life, understand the promise each hopes a wedding communicates, and identify marriage-level questions deserving conversation. Marriage meaning precedes celebration design. Concrete private reflection precedes disclosure; prediction is perspective-taking rather than a test; differences are information; understanding, pausing and non-agreement are valid outcomes.

## User flow
Private reflection → sharing approval → waiting → approved-content bridge → grounding → marriage sentence and listening → editable Mirror → simultaneous promise reveal → ordinary-life discussion → two-good-purposes tradeoff → optional faith discernment → pause check → neutral capture → private integration → shared record → pilot feedback.

## Private prompts
1. **Marriage in One Sentence** (`marriage-sentence`)
2. **An Ordinary Evening** (`ten-year-words`), with up to three words
3. **The Promise** (`promise`)
4. **What Still Needs Conversation?** (`marriage-open-question`)

Every response belongs to one partner. Private is the default; exact wording or a locally edited summary enters shared mode only through explicit approval. The open question is excluded from bridge rules, preventing its existence from being inferred.

## Shared encounter and record
The shared encounter reveals only approved wording or invites speaking aloud. Simultaneous Reveal hides both predictions until both partners confirm readiness, then reveals both without scoring. The record contains the couple’s shared value, meaningful difference, open question, improved understanding, approved Mirror wording and agreed next step—never private integration or predictions.

## Christ-Centered behavior
Christ-Centered mode adds static Scripture, prayer, a Christ-centered word/promise option and a discernment step about Scripture, church teaching, conscience, experience, counsel and hope. It never translates preference into divine command or privileges a modern wedding form. Core mode skips discernment in both directions.

## Removed from v0.1
The former legal/timing and destination-celebration comparison was intentionally moved out of Encounter One into a possible later pathway. No wedding structure is evaluated here.

## Outside scope
Production privacy, authentication, synchronization, Firebase, AI, analytics, deployment, payments, dashboards, Meaning Maps, Decision Studio and Encounter Two remain outside scope.

## Pilot hypotheses
Test whether sentence completion is concrete enough; three-word selection produces useful stories; prediction creates curiosity rather than pressure; the tradeoff reveals meaning without pushing a format; privacy boundaries are understood; faith content deepens humility; and the encounter feels guided rather than questionnaire-like.
