# Encounter Two v0.1 — What Are We Trying to Preserve?

## Purpose and scope

Encounter Two helps each partner notice what they want the wedding-planning season and eventual celebration to protect before they compare wedding structures. It is a one-device, localStorage-backed prototype for testing the experience—not a decision tool or production privacy system.

## User flow

1. Each partner privately chooses up to three preservation values, identifies a priority, and notices outside pressure.
2. Each partner explicitly decides what may enter the shared conversation.
3. Together, the couple starts lightly, predicts and reveals priorities without scoring, tells a story, explores the meaning beneath a priority, and considers a competing-goods tradeoff.
4. Christ-Centered journeys include an additional faith-and-conscience discernment step; Core journeys skip it in both directions.
5. The couple preserves a small shared record, then each partner checks out privately.

## Private prompts and privacy rules

Encounter Two uses unique prompt IDs in the existing partner response maps: `e2-preserve-values`, `e2-top-priority`, `e2-pressure`, and `e2-private-checkout`. Partner responses never enter Together mode merely because they exist. Pressure defaults to private and appears only when its owner explicitly chooses **Bring this into our conversation**. Private checkout responses are never read by the shared record.

## Shared conversation

The shared sequence is Start Light, Predict and Reveal, Story, Meaning, Tradeoff, optional Christ-Centered Discernment, and Capture. Prediction is perspective-taking: the interface awards no points and shows no winner, correctness label, percentage, compatibility result, or recommendation.

## Christ-Centered behavior

Christ-Centered mode may surface Scripture, church teaching, conscience, stewardship, prayer, wise counsel, and requirements that should be verified. It never converts a preference into a command from God or labels one modern wedding format as more faithful.

## Shared record

The record contains only the shared priority, a stronger individual emphasis the couple chose to preserve, a shared tension, and the agreed next step. It links back to the Encounter One record. Encounter Three is named only as unavailable.

## Intentionally not built

This revision does not add wedding-location, guest-count, exact-budget, marriage-timing, ceremony-structure, or one-event-versus-multiple-event decisions. It also excludes Encounter Three, Decision Studio, matrices, the full Meaning Map, AI, Firebase, authentication, analytics, payments, deployment, and real multi-device privacy.

## Pilot hypotheses

- Value cards feel easier than written reflection.
- Prediction increases curiosity rather than competition.
- The playful opener lowers tension.
- Story prompts reveal meaning better than abstract classification.
- Tradeoff discussion reveals competing goods without forcing a choice.
- Couples can name meaningful differences without feeling scored.
- The encounter feels conversational rather than homework-like.
